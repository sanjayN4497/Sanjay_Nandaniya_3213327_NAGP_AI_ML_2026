# Essential visitor information

Singapore has a tropical climate, with typical temperatures described by the tourism board as about 25–33°C. Light, airy clothing and an umbrella are useful because rainfall can occur. Singapore Standard Time is GMT+8. English is widely used, and Mandarin Chinese, Malay and Tamil are also commonly spoken.

Visitors should verify the current entry and visa rules using official government channels, rather than relying on a static travel guide. Wireless@SGx hotspots can be used by visitors with eligible foreign mobile numbers through the Wireless@SGx app. Tourist tax-refund eligibility and conditions should be checked with participating retailers and official guidance.

# Transport and accessibility

The MRT and public bus network are extensive. Payment options described by the tourism board include contactless bank cards, mobile wallets, EZ-Link cards, cash and the Singapore Tourist Pass. MRT stations and public buses are wheelchair accessible, and open strollers are allowed. Check current fares, service changes and pass conditions before travel.

# Respectful travel

Smoking is restricted in many indoor and public-transport-related areas. Prayer facilities are available at major hotels and attractions and mosques are accessible around the city; travellers should verify arrangements with the individual operator.
