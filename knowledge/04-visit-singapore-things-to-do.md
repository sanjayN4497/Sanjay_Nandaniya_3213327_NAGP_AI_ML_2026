# Activities by visitor interest

Visit Singapore groups things to do into nature, culture and heritage, architecture, family activities, nightlife, museums and galleries, shopping, dining, and unusual experiences. These categories help travellers choose activities to suit their interests.

# Culture and neighbourhood exploration

The official site's neighbourhood highlights associate the Civic District with colonial architecture and National Gallery Singapore. Kampong Gelam combines Malay heritage with street culture. Little India offers shops selling spices and textiles alongside street art. Chinatown features temples, murals and traditional merchants.

# Nature and evening activities

Mandai offers nature and wildlife experiences at Mandai Wildlife Reserve. Bird Paradise is a bird-focused attraction. The Singapore River offers river cruises and nightlife.

# Planning suggestions

For art and heritage interests, consider museums and a cultural neighbourhood. For nature or family interests, consider a wildlife activity. For an evening outing, consider the river area. These are planning suggestions, not official itineraries. Check operators for opening hours, tickets, accessibility and age restrictions; this summary does not establish current availability or prices.
