# Itinerary building blocks

Singapore itineraries work well when each day is built around one connected area and allows breaks from heat or rain. Marina Bay supports a mix of outdoor waterfront time and sheltered attractions. The civic district and Bras Basah have museums and historic places. Chinatown, Little India and Kampong Glam can form a culture-and-food day. Gardens, the Botanic Gardens, the East Coast, Sentosa and Mandai offer outdoor or nature-focused options.

# Rain-aware alternatives

For rainy or very hot periods, prefer museum visits in the civic/central area, sheltered dining at hawker centres or restaurants, and indoor attraction elements. Keep outdoor waterfront walks, gardens, beaches and nature walks flexible. This is an itinerary-planning suggestion, not a weather forecast: use the live weather result for the relevant dates.

# Families and food

Families can consider wildlife attractions in the Mandai area, gardens and attractions around Sentosa, while planning age-appropriate durations and checking attraction restrictions directly. Food experiences can be planned around hawker centres and the cultural districts, grouping meals with nearby activities to reduce unnecessary travel.
